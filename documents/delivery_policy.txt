E-COMMERCE DELIVERY OPERATIONS POLICY

1. Delivery Risk Management

Orders identified as having a high probability of late delivery should be reviewed by the operations team before the estimated delivery date.

2. Seller Performance

Sellers with a history of late deliveries should receive additional monitoring.

If a seller's historical late-delivery rate is consistently high, the operations team should investigate the seller's recent orders and delivery performance.

3. Geographic Risk

Orders involving sellers and customers located in different states may require additional monitoring because cross-state delivery can involve longer transportation distances and additional logistics steps.

4. High-Risk Orders

Orders with multiple risk indicators should receive higher priority during operational review.

Risk indicators may include:

- High seller historical late-delivery rate
- Short estimated delivery time
- High freight cost
- Cross-state seller/customer location
- Multiple items or sellers involved in an order

5. Recommended Operational Actions

For high-risk orders, the operations team may:

- Review the seller's recent delivery performance
- Contact the seller for an updated fulfillment status
- Verify the expected delivery date
- Prioritize monitoring of the shipment
- Escalate significant delivery risks to the operations manager

6. Human Approval

Important operational actions that affect customers, sellers, or orders should require human approval before execution.

The AI system may investigate problems and recommend actions, but it should not independently execute consequential operational actions without human approval.
